**SUBJECT:** 14-Day Intervention to Plug ₹61.6L/Month Demo No-Show Leak  

---

### 1. The Core Problem & Financial Sizing (The ₹ Number)
Analysis of 5,000 leads across June–July 2026 reveals BrightChamps converts only **7.24% of leads**, leaving ₹2.81 Cr on the table every 60 days. The single costliest bottleneck is **Leak 2: Demo No-Shows**.
- **36.20% of parents who schedule a demo fail to attend** (1,169 leads / 584.5 leads/month).
- Sized at the baseline conversion rate of attendees (17.57%) and ₹60,000 package price, **demo no-shows bleed ₹61,62,786 per month** in lost revenue.
- Root cause: **Scheduling latency and friction.** Attendance collapses from **74.1%** when scheduled within 24h to **43.5%** after 48h. Parents simply forget or encounter scheduling conflicts.

---

### 2. The Proposed Lever (Go-Live in < 10 Days, No Engineering Sprint)
We propose deploying an **Autonomous WhatsApp "No-Show Concierge" & Instant Rescheduler**:
- **Mechanism:** Triggered via webhook 15 minutes after a missed demo. An AI concierge messages the parent via WhatsApp in their local timezone with an empathetic, personalized check-in and interactive one-tap rescheduling slots.
- **Why It Works:** It eliminates phone-tag friction, operates 24/7 across US, UK, and SEA timezones, and recaptures high-intent parents while motivation is still fresh.
- **Financial Return:** Recovering just **20% of no-shows** adds **+117 attendees and +20.5 conversions/month**, delivering **+₹12,32,550/month in net gross revenue** against a tool cost of <₹25,000/month (**>4,800% ROI**).

---

### 3. Baseline Metrics to Register Before Day 1
Before turning on the workflow, the following hard baselines must be locked:
1. **Demo No-Show Rate:** **36.20%** (Benchmark over last 60 days: 1,169 no-shows / 3,229 scheduled).
2. **Post-No-Show Reschedule Rate:** Current organic recovery rate of missed demos (estimated **< 5%**).
3. **Demo Join Rate:** **63.80%** of scheduled demos.
4. **Overall Scheduled-to-Conversion Rate:** **11.21%**.

---

### 4. How Success Will Be Measured (The Scorecard)
We will track a weekly operational scorecard against target thresholds:
- **Primary Metric:** **Demo No-Show Rate** $\rightarrow$ Target reduction from **36.2% to < 27.0%** (a 25% relative reduction).
- **Secondary Metric:** **Automated Reschedule Conversion** $\rightarrow$ Target **$\ge$ 20%** of contacted no-shows re-booking and attending.
- **Financial Metric:** **Net New Monthly Revenue** $\rightarrow$ Target **+₹10,00,000 to +₹12,00,000/month**.
- **Guardrail Metric:** WhatsApp Opt-out / Block rate kept below **1.5%**.

---

### 5. Biggest Adoption Risk & Mitigation Strategy
- **The Risk:** **Rep Resistance & Process Non-Compliance.**
  If sales reps fail to mark attendance in real-time or perceive the automated agent as "stealing" their commissions or interfering with their parent relationship, the webhook trigger fails.
- **The Mitigation:**
  1. **Zero Added Effort:** The rep changes nothing except ticking "No Show" in the CRM (which they already do).
  2. **Commission Protection:** 100% of the commission on recovered demos remains attributed to the assigned rep. The bot acts as the rep’s personal 24/7 assistant, not their competitor.
  3. **Executive Incentive:** Highlight top rep recoveries in weekly sales all-hands meetings.
